# 📝 Google Play Store Listing & Metadata Guide

## 1. Character Limitations & Policy Rules

| Field | Max Length | Guidelines |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **App Title** | **30 chars** | Avoid generic keywords, emoji, or ALL CAPS. Must represent official brand name. |
| **Short Description** | **80 chars** | First copy visible in store search results and product page above the fold. |
| **Full Description** | **4,000 chars** | Detailed app feature description, feature highlights, and terms. |

---

## 2. Prohibited Metadata Practices
- **Keyword Stuffing**: Do not repeat terms repeatedly or create lists of unrelated search keywords.
- **Misleading Testimonials**: Do not include anonymous user review quotes or claim "Best App 2026" without official verification.
- **Prohibited Characters**: Avoid special characters like emojis or ASCII art in app titles.
- **Price Information**: Do not mention pricing details ("Free", "On Sale", "Discount") in the title or short description.
